title: "Writing a lab report that gets marks"
date: 2026-11-04
tag: "Labs"
summary: "What each section needs, in the order markers check it."
steps:
  - "Read the marking rubric before you start writing"
  - "Write the objective in one or two sentences"
  - "Describe the procedure briefly; reference the lab manual instead of copying it"
  - "Present results in labelled tables and figures with units"
  - "Include a sample calculation for every formula used"
  - "Compare results to theory and give the percent error"
  - "Explain sources of error specifically (not just 'human error')"
  - "Write the conclusion last and answer the objective directly"
