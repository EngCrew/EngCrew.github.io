About

Why it's called EngCrew

I started EngCrew with no crew at all. I was my own writer, editor and director, and I learned every one of those jobs by getting it wrong first.

So the name was never about me. When a video opens with "Hey there EngCrew!", it's talking to you: the students watching, building and figuring engineering out alongside each other. You're the crew.

The content follows the same idea. I take real engineering work like circuit boards, 3D printing and hands-on builds and make it something anyone can follow, along with the study and co-op advice that gets you through the degree.

"Limitations are the backbone of innovations."
