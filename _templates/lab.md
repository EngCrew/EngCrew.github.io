  Lab title · Course code · Lab section · Your name & student # · Partners · Date

  Key equations with variables defined. Cite the lab manual or textbook.

  Equipment list. Brief procedure; reference the manual instead of copying it.

  Table 1: [Title] (with units)
  Figure 1: [Title] (labelled axes, units, legend)
  Sample calculations for each formula.

  5. DISCUSSION
  Compare results to theory · Percent error · Specific sources of error · How to improve

  6. CONCLUSION
  Answer the objective directly using your key result.

  REFERENCES
  [Format required by your course]

  Raw data · Extra calculations
