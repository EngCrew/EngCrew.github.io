Most first-year engineering students hit a wall around the first midterms. The content isn't impossible; the volume is. A system beats motivation.

## Do problems, not notes

Rereading slides feels productive but rarely sticks. Spend most of your study time solving practice problems, then check where you got stuck.

## Use the weekly reset

Every Sunday, list each course's deliverables for the week: problem sets, labs, quizzes. Block time for each before the week starts.

## Go to office hours early

Office hours in week 3 are quiet. In week 7 they're packed. Show up early with one specific question.

## Protect sleep

An all-nighter before a midterm usually costs more marks than the extra review earns. Stop studying at a set time the night before.
