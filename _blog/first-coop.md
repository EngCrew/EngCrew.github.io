Your first co-op search can feel impossible: every posting wants experience, and you can't get experience without a job. The good news is that recruiters hiring first and second-year students expect a thin resume. They're looking for proof you can learn fast and work with people.

## 1. Lead with projects, not jobs

Course design projects, robotics or Formula/Baja teams, hackathons, and even a personal Arduino build all count. Describe what you built, which tools you used, and what the result was.

## 2. Apply wide and early

Treat it like a numbers game. Many students apply to 40+ postings before landing an offer. Set a weekly target and track every application in a spreadsheet.

## 3. Tailor the top third

You don't need a new resume for every job. Swap your skills line and your top project so they match the posting's keywords.

## 4. Practise the behavioural interview

Most co-op interviews ask "tell me about a time when..." questions. Prepare 4–5 stories using the STAR format (Situation, Task, Action, Result) and reuse them.
