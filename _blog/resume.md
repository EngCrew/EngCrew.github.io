draft: false
---
1. **Listing duties instead of results.** "Responsible for testing" becomes "Tested 12 circuit prototypes and cut failure rate by 30%".
1. **Burying the skills.** Put software and tools (SolidWorks, MATLAB, Python, AutoCAD) near the top where they're easy to scan.
1. **Going over one page.** As a student, one page is almost always enough.
1. **Fancy formatting.** Columns, icons and graphics can confuse applicant tracking systems. A clean single-column layout reads best.
1. **No numbers.** Quantify wherever you can: team size, budget, time saved, parts made.
