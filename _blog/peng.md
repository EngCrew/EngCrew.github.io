In Canada, "Professional Engineer" is a protected title regulated by each province or territory. The steps are broadly similar everywhere, though the details vary.

## The general path

1. Graduate from an accredited engineering program (most Canadian programs are accredited by Engineers Canada's CEAB).
1. Register as an Engineer-in-Training (EIT) with your provincial regulator. Many let you start right after graduation.
1. Build supervised engineering work experience, usually around four years.
1. Pass the professional practice exam on law and ethics.
1. Apply for your P.Eng licence.

> Rules differ by province (for example PEO in Ontario, APEGA in Alberta, OIQ in Québec). Always check your regulator's website for the current requirements.
